Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to Ettazenn. We are committed to protecting your personal data, respecting your privacy rights, and maintaining complete transparency regarding how we handle your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, mobile application, or interact with our holistic balance coaching services.

2 Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide you with seamless wellness experiences and tailored coaching insights:

  • Personal Identity Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and account credentials when you register or submit a contact inquiry.
  • Health & Lifestyle Metrics: Voluntary wellness parameters, dietary preferences, activity logs, and coach notes shared during personal onboarding.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, mobile device identifiers, operating system data, and app interaction logs.
  • Payment Data: Securely encrypted transaction logs handled directly by certified third-party payment gateways.

3 How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is strictly processed to optimize your wellness journey and deliver high-quality platform services, including:

  • Delivering customized coaching programs and app features tailored to your targets.
  • Facilitating direct communication between you and certified Ettazenn coaches.
  • Responding to support requests, user inquiries, and feedback promptly.
  • Enhancing application security, preventing fraud, and ensuring platform reliability.
  • Sending critical updates, newsletters, and promotional offerings (with full opt-out choices).

4 Data Sharing and Disclosure

Ettazenn does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary data under strict confidentiality agreements with:

  • Verified Service Providers: Cloud hosting, analytics partners, and communication infrastructure providers who strictly operate under encryption protocols.
  • Legal Requirements: When compelled by applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority to comply with legal obligations.

5 Data Security & Retention

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures—including TLS encryption, access control frameworks, and periodic audits—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services.

6 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific privacy rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Rectification: Request a copy of your personal data or ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): Request the permanent removal of your account and associated personal data.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link or account settings.
